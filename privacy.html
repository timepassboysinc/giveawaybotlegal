DMall Giveaway Bot — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DMall Giveaway Bot (“the Bot”) may process information when used in Discord servers. Please read it before using the Bot.

1. Information processed

To provide giveaway functionality, the Bot may process Discord server IDs, channel IDs, message IDs, user IDs, role IDs, giveaway settings (such as prize, duration, host, and winner count), entry records, and giveaway results. The precise information processed depends on the features enabled and how the Bot is configured.

The Bot is not intended to collect users’ passwords, payment-card details, private messages, or sensitive personal information. Do not submit sensitive information to the Bot.

2. Message content

If a feature or command requires reading message content, the Bot may process the relevant message text to respond to that command or perform that feature. Message content should not be collected or retained for unrelated purposes. If the deployed version does not use message content, remove the Message Content privileged intent and this paragraph should be revised to reflect that.

3. How information is used

Information is used to operate and maintain giveaways, record entries, check eligibility where configured, select and announce winners, enforce Bot permissions, troubleshoot errors, prevent abuse, and provide support.

4. Storage and retention

Some giveaway information may be stored so that active giveaways can continue after a restart and so that results can be maintained. Information should be retained only for as long as needed for these purposes, then deleted or anonymized when no longer required. Actual retention periods depend on the Bot’s hosting and database configuration.

5. Sharing

Information may be processed by Discord as part of the platform and by the hosting or database providers used to operate the Bot. It is not intended to be sold. Information may be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the Bot, its users, or others.

6. Your choices and requests

You can stop using the Bot by leaving a server where it is installed or asking a server administrator to remove it. For a request to access, correct, or delete information associated with your use of the Bot, contact the Bot operator through the official DMall support server. Some records may need to be retained for operational, security, or legal reasons.

7. Children

The Bot is not designed to collect personal information from children. Users should follow Discord’s minimum age requirements and the laws applicable in their location.

8. Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect stored information. No online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

This Policy may be updated when the Bot’s features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at the same URL with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Bot operator through the official DMall support server or the contact method listed on the Bot’s Discord application page.
